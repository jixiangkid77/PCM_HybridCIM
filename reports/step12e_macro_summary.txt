10. PCM-CIM macro estimation via NeuroSim  (12e-3)
================================================================
Method:  NeuroSim Inference_pytorch C++ backend, standalone mode
         (PyTorch frontend bypassed; LeNet-5 functional correctness
         already verified by RTL/Python bit-exact tests).
Tool:    Modified NeuroSim main.cpp (early `return 0` removed to
         enable latency/energy path; KID-added area print preserved).
         Build at ~/NeuroSim/Inference_pytorch/NeuroSIM/main
         (566 KB ELF, May 14 2026 02:13).
Tech:    22 nm CMOS (Param.cpp technode=22)
         PCM device parameters: Ron=3 kohm, Roff=3 Mohm,
         on/off ratio = 1000, writeVoltage=3 V
         widthInFeatureSize1T1R=24, heightInFeatureSize1T1R=4
Mapping: Conventional (novelMapping=false)
         subArray = 64 x 64 (smallest size that fits LeNet-5 in
         NeuroSim hierarchy: needs maxTileSize >= 4 x subArray)
ADC:     MultilevelSenseAmp, 32 levels = 5-bit (param->levelOutput)
         numColMuxed = 8 (8 cols share 1 ADC)

Network: LeNet-5 (61,470 total weights), described in
         reports/12e_neurosim/configs/NetWork_LeNet5.csv:
           conv1: 32x32x1  -> 5x5 -> 6  (150 weights)
           conv2: 16x16x6  -> 5x5 -> 16 (2400 weights)
           fc1:   400 -> 120              (48000 weights)
           fc2:   120 -> 84               (10080 weights)
           fc3:   84  -> 10               (840 weights)
         NeuroSim assumes same-padding: introduces ~30 % ops
         inflation vs. true LeNet-5 valid-padding (28x28 conv1
         output). Acceptable approximation for area; energy
         and latency are conservative upper bounds.

Trace:   Random INT8-quantized weights + bit-serial random
         activations (smoke trace). For final paper energy,
         real activation traces from Paper 1 PyTorch golden
         pipeline are required (deferred to next refinement).

PCM encoding sensitivity sweep (4 schemes)
-------------------------------------------
NeuroSim natively supports cellBit in {1, 2, 4, 8}. To cover
4 encoding schemes (single-ended/differential x sign-magnitude
embedded/external), 3 NeuroSim runs were performed and post-
processed:

  cellBit=1:  ChipArea 3.455 mm^2, PCM array 0.085, ADC 1.502
  cellBit=2:  ChipArea 2.522 mm^2, PCM array 0.061, ADC 1.073
  cellBit=4:  ChipArea 0.872 mm^2, PCM array 0.018, ADC 0.322
     (all 3 runs at subArray=64, conventional mapping, 22nm)

For each encoding scheme, PCM array and ADC area were scaled by
  factor = (final_cells_per_INT8) / (NeuroSim_native_cells_per_INT8)
to model differential pair (factor=2) and sign-magnitude (factor
=(N+1)/N for the extra sign cell). All weight-count-dependent
quantities were further scaled by the PCM weight ratio:
  PCM ratio = 58,396 / 61,470 = 0.9500
(58,396 PCM-side weights vs 61,470 total LeNet-5, with the
remaining 3,074 weights stored in SRAM under layer-aware
protection; see Section 7 of step12c_synthesis_summary_lanesliced.txt).

  Scheme  Encoding              Cells   Factor   PCM-CIM area   Latency   Energy
                                /INT8                  (mm^2)      (us)     (nJ)
  -----   --------------------  ----- --------   ------------   -------   ------
  I       1-bit + sign-mag        9    1.125 (+1)    1.696       115.7    461.2
  II      2-bit differential (*)  8    2.000 (x2)    2.154       102.6    550.8
  III     2-bit + sign-mag        5    1.250 (+1)    1.346       102.6    344.3
  IV      4-bit differential      4    2.000 (x2)    0.646       131.8    386.5

  (*) Scheme II is the paper baseline (IBM 2018 Nature precedent).
      Schemes I, III, IV form the sensitivity range.

  PCM-CIM = PCM array + ADC only. NeuroSim's reported Accumulation,
  IC, and Other components are NOT added to C, because they overlap
  with Yosys digital (Step 12c, A) and OpenRAM SRAM (Step 12e-1, B);
  including them would double-count.

  Energy includes only PCM-side dynamic energy (read). SRAM dynamic
  energy is deferred to SPICE (Section 8 caveat).

11. Final macro summary  A + B + C  (12e-4)
================================================================
Putting together the three estimation paths:

  A = Yosys digital periphery       (Step 12c, sky130A bit-exact)
  B = OpenRAM SRAM and ROM macros   (Step 12e-1/-2/-2b, sky130A)
  C = NeuroSim PCM-CIM macro        (Step 12e-3, NeuroSim 22nm)

Note: A and B are sky130 (130 nm-derived rules under sky130A),
C is NeuroSim 22 nm (NeuroSim's native PDK abstraction). A direct
sum is technology-mixed; this is acknowledged as a limitation.
For paper rigor, the components are reported separately, and the
total is given as an estimate for system-level discussion.

Component breakdown
-------------------
                                                Lower      Upper
                                               (mm^2)     (mm^2)
  A. Digital periphery (Yosys, Step 12c)        1.263      1.263
                                                ----------------
  B. Conventional SRAM + ROM (sky130A):
     B.1 sram_pool                              0.281      7.025
         (storage-only lower / 25-replicated upper, PENDING 5.4)
     B.2 feature_buffer (2 banks)               0.669      0.669
     B.3 addr_decoder x 50 lanes                3.984      3.984
     B.4 bias_storage / drift_lut_exp2          0.0215     0.0215
     B.5 drift_lut_log2                         0.0373     0.0373
     B.6 nu_pcm_pool ROM (if HW, +5.2)          0          0.089
         (0 if simulation-only; ~0.089 mm^2 if 467 Kbit ROM @ 0.19
          um^2/bit derived from addr_decoder per-bit density)
     ----------------------------------------- ------     ------
     B subtotal                                 4.956     11.835

  C. PCM-CIM macro (NeuroSim, by scheme):
     C.I   1-bit + sign-mag                     1.696      1.696
     C.II  2-bit differential (paper baseline)  2.154      2.154
     C.III 2-bit + sign-mag                     1.346      1.346
     C.IV  4-bit differential                   0.646      0.646
                                                ------     ------
     C range (sensitivity)                      0.646      2.154

A + B + C  (using C.II baseline):
  Lower:  1.263 + 4.956 + 2.154 =  8.373 mm^2
  Upper:  1.263 + 11.835 + 2.154 = 15.252 mm^2

A + B + C  (full sensitivity range):
  Min (Scheme IV, lower B):   1.263 + 4.956 + 0.646 =  6.865 mm^2
  Max (Scheme II, upper B):   1.263 + 11.835 + 2.154 = 15.252 mm^2

Latency (per image):
  RTL cycle-accurate (Step 9, 100 MHz):  6.18 ms (617,573 cycles)
  PCM-CIM macro access (NeuroSim):       102.6 - 131.8 us (per scheme)
  Note: These two latencies are at different levels of abstraction
  (RTL controls cycle-by-cycle execution; NeuroSim reports macro
  access time) and should not be directly compared.

Energy (PCM-CIM dynamic, per image):
  Scheme II baseline:  ~551 nJ  (NeuroSim, dummy random trace)
  Sensitivity range:   344 - 551 nJ
  Refinement needed:   Replace dummy random activation with real
                       traces from Paper 1 PyTorch golden pipeline
                       (post-conv, post-ReLU activation maps from
                       MNIST test set).

What this section DOES NOT cover
---------------------------------
  - SRAM dynamic Read/Write energy (broken in OpenRAM analytical
    output; SPICE characterization deferred; see Section 8 caveat)
  - ROM timing and power (Section 9 policy: area-only)
  - System-level pipelining, off-chip I/O, NoC overhead beyond what
    Yosys + NeuroSim individually report
  - Layout-level effects (routing congestion, power grid, decap)
