NOTE on lane-sliced refactor (verified 2026-05-12 21:12 JST):
The address-mapping decoder modules (sram_addr_decoder, pcm_addr_decoder)
gained a per-instance LANE_ID parameter, allowing each of the 25 SIMD
lanes to load only its share of the layer protection maps. A full
sky130 re-synthesis (Yosys 0.33, 19m42s wall, peak RAM 23.53 GB) of
the post-refactor RTL produces a top-level area of
  1,263,370.422399 um^2
which is BIT-EXACT IDENTICAL (to six decimal places) to the pre-refactor
baseline preserved at reports/baseline_pre_lanesliced/. Both runs report
the same 241 benign warnings across 29 unique messages and the same
sub-module area breakdown. This confirms that the lane-sliced refactor
incurs ZERO digital-periphery hardware cost: the .LANE_ID(gi) parameter
propagation is absorbed as a compile-time paramod variant on a
black-box module boundary and generates no additional cells or wires.

Warning audit (29 unique, 241 total) - ALL BENIGN

Category 1: ABC informational (2)
  - 9 multi-output gates skipped (fa_1, etc.); ABC used single-
    output cell combinations instead. Minor area cost (<5%).
  - "network is combinational" suggestion for fraig pass.

Category 2: Scan-DFF cells skipped (24, by cell variant)
  - sky130 scan-DFF (sdfxx_*) cells have D&!SCE|SCD&SCE pin
    function that Yosys cannot parse. dfflibmap falls back to
    non-scan DFF variants. Design has no scan chain, so no impact.

Category 3: Small-memory to register replacement (3)
  - cfg_mem (layer_config_regfile.v): correctly inferred as FFs
  - mem (logit_rf.v): correctly inferred as FFs
  - sram_pipe (combiner.v): correctly inferred as FFs
  All three are inherently register files, NOT SRAM macros.
